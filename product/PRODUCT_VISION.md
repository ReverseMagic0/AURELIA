# Product vision

AURELIA enables a small team to build multiple polished, reliable, native-feeling Flutter applications without repeatedly reinventing foundational engineering and design decisions.

## Outcomes
- Consistent engineering and visual quality across applications.
- Fast product delivery without sacrificing correctness or maintainability.
- A distinctive, coherent design language rather than default component-library appearance.
- Predictable behavior across iOS, Android, English, Arabic, RTL, light, and dark modes.
- Quality supported by repeatable automated and device-level evidence.

## Product principle
AURELIA should remove repeated work and reduce avoidable defects. It must not become a generic framework whose maintenance burden exceeds the value it provides.
